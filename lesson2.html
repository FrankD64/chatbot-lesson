Make the Computer Listen

In Lesson 1, your programs only talked. Now you’ll make one that asks a question, remembers the answer and uses it.

input() asks a question

Codeinput("What is your name? ")

When Python reaches this line, it shows the question and waits for you to type an answer and press Enter.

Leave a space before the last quote so the answer doesn’t touch the question.

A variable is a labelled box

Codename = input("What is your name? ")

The = means “put the answer in the box called name.” If you type Sam, the box holds "Sam".

Use the box in a message

Codename = input("What is your name? ") print("Hi " + name)
OutputWhat is your name? Sam Hi Sam

The + joins pieces of text together into one message.

No quotes around the box’s name

✓ Prints the answer
print(name)

Shows what’s inside the box: Sam

✗ Prints the word
print("name")

Shows the word itself: name

Mind the space

✗ No space
print("Hi" + name)

Output: HiSam

✓ Space inside the quotes
print("Hi " + name)

Output: Hi Sam

Python only adds the spaces you type.

Two questions, two boxes

Codename = input("What is your name? ") food = input("What is your favourite food? ") print("Hi " + name + "! I love " + food + " too!")
OutputWhat is your name? Sam What is your favourite food? tacos Hi Sam! I love tacos too!

Each answer gets its own variable with its own name.

Step 3 of 4 · 20 minutes · Activity 3

Make it: Chatbot Builder

Pick a topic for your chatbot: food, games, animals, sports, music, or your own idea.

  1. Press ▶ Run, type an answer in the box that appears, and press Enter.
  2. Change the question to something else (for example, “What should I call you? ”).
  3. Add a second question about your topic. Save the answer in a new variable, such as game or animal.
  4. Change the print() line so your chatbot’s reply uses both answers.
  5. Explain your code. Above each line, add a comment that starts with # and says what the line does. Python skips comments, so they won’t break anything.
Stuck?

Copy the pattern of the first input() line onto a new line, change the variable name and the question, then add your new variable into the print() line with +.

Seeing HiSam? Add a space inside the quotes. Seeing the word name instead of the answer? Take the quotes off the variable.

Prefer not to type your explanation? You can record your voice explaining each line, or take a screenshot and draw arrows to label each line.
